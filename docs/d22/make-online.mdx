# D22 — Make It Online on Traqcare

By the end of this page your D22 is **online on Traqcare** and you can watch its live video.

**You will need:**

- The D22, with the SIM card and memory card inserted and power connected (see [Installation](./installation.md)).
- The **Device ID**, printed on the label on the D22 or on its box.
- A smartphone with the **Traqcam Connect** app (free on the App Store and Google Play).
- A Traqcare login at [track.traqcare.com](https://track.traqcare.com). **Coins** to activate the device are helpful but not required now: you can activate later (see Step 4).

## The steps at a glance

| Step | What you do | Where |
|:----:|-------------|-------|
| 1 | Connect your phone to the D22's Wi-Fi | Phone |
| 2 | Log in to the D22 | Traqcam Connect |
| 3 | Point the D22 at Traqcare | Traqcam Connect |
| 4 | Add the D22 to your account | Traqcare |
| 5 | Turn on the camera sensors | Traqcare |
| 6 | Set up the cameras | Traqcare |
| 7 | Check that it's online and watch live video | Traqcare |

## Step 1 — Connect your phone to the D22's Wi-Fi

The D22 turns on its own Wi-Fi automatically when it powers up.

1. On your phone, open **Settings → Wi-Fi**.
2. Find the network whose name is the D22's **IMEI number** (a long number, for example `568080023426`).
3. Connect with the password **`88888888`** (eight 8s).

:::note "No internet connection" is normal
Your phone may say the D22's Wi-Fi has **no internet**. That's expected, so stay connected. If your phone keeps switching back to mobile data or another Wi-Fi, turn off mobile data until you finish Step 3.
:::

## Step 2 — Log in to the D22 in Traqcam Connect

1. Open **Traqcam Connect**.
2. Tap **Settings** at the bottom of the screen.
3. Enter the password **`111111`** (six 1s) and tap **Sign In**.

The settings page opens with the device name **WD02** at the top. This is your D22.

## Step 3 — Point the D22 at Traqcare

1. Under **Network Settings**, tap **Center Server**.
2. Tap **Center Server1**.
3. Set these values:

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Protocol** | **JT808-2013** (shown as `JT808-2013-M1`) |
| **Server IP** | `d4.traqcare.com` |
| **Server Port** | `5101` |
| Gps Interval | Leave as it is (`30`) |

4. Tap **Save**.

You can now disconnect your phone from the D22's Wi-Fi.

## Step 4 — Add the D22 to your Traqcare account

1. Log in at [track.traqcare.com](https://track.traqcare.com).
2. Go to **Settings** (gear icon) → **Devices** → **Add Device**.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/d22/traqcare-devices.png" alt="Traqcare Settings, Devices tab with the Add Device button highlighted" width={720} />
</Shots>

3. Fill in the **Device Details** tab:

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Client** | The account that will own the D22 |
| **IMEI / Unique ID** | The **Device ID** from the label, **without the leading 0** (see the box below) |
| **Protocol / Port** | **JT808 (5101)** |
| **Model Name** | `D22` |
| **Device Type** | **Wired** |
| **Device Timezone** | The vehicle's time zone |
| **Object Name** | A name for the vehicle, anything you like |
| **Object Type** | **Car** (or the right vehicle type) |
| **Subscription Term** | Choose a plan if you see one. Coins are used to activate the device. **If the list is empty, leave it blank**: see the note below. |

:::warning Remove the leading 0
If the Device ID starts with **0**, leave out that first 0. Traqcare never uses the leading 0, here or in Camera Settings (Step 6).

| Device ID on the label | Enter in IMEI / Unique ID |
|------------------------|---------------------------|
| `012564542389` | `12564542389` |
:::

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/d22/traqcare-add-device.png" alt="Add Device form, Device Details tab, with protocol JT808 (5101) and model D22" width={620} />
</Shots>

:::info No plans in "Subscription Term"? You can activate later
Each plan in the list shows how many coins you have, for example *1 Year (Available Coins: 20)*. **Most new accounts have no coins yet, so the list is empty.** That's fine:

1. Leave **Subscription Term** blank and finish adding the device (Steps 5 and 6). The D22 is saved in your account but **not activated yet**.
2. **Get coins:** ask your supplier (Skywonder or the reseller you bought the D22 from) to add coins to your account.
3. **Activate:** go to **Settings → Devices**, find your D22 and click **Activate** in its row. Choose a plan and confirm.

If you don't see an **Activate** link, your supplier can activate the device for you.

The D22 needs to be **activated** before it shows up as online and before you can use it on Traqcare.
:::

## Step 5 — Turn on the camera sensors

Open the **Device Sensors** tab and switch these **on**:

| Sensor | Parameter | Reading Type |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| **IGNITION** | `acc` | Direct |
| **POWER** | `power` | Direct |
| **CAMERA** | — | — |
| **VIDEO CAMERA** | — | — |

Leave the other sensors off.

## Step 6 — Set up the cameras

When **VIDEO CAMERA** is on, a **Camera Settings** tab appears. Open it and fill in:

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Number of Channels** | **2** for a standard D22 (2 cameras). Choose **4** if your D22 version has 3 or 4 cameras. |
| **DeviceId** | The **same Device ID you entered in Step 4**, **without the leading 0** |
| **Video Platform** | **Traqcare** |

Click **Submit**. If you chose a plan in Step 4, the coins are used and the D22 is activated. If you didn't, activate it later (see the note in Step 4).

## Step 7 — Check that it's online and watch live video

1. Open the **Map View** in Traqcare.
2. Find your D22 in the vehicle list. Within a few minutes it turns **online** (coloured instead of grey).
3. Click the vehicle, then click the **camera icon** to open live video.

If you see live video from the cameras, **your D22 is set up correctly**. 🎉

Not online after 5 minutes? See [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md#d22-is-not-online-on-traqcare).

**Next:** [Test Your D22](./test-device.mdx). It takes about 10 minutes and checks everything works.
