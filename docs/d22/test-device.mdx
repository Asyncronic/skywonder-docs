# D22 — Test Your D22

Use this checklist after [Make It Online on Traqcare](./make-online.mdx). It takes about **10 minutes**. You can do it on a desk with a 12 V power supply or in the vehicle.

| # | Test | How to do it | ✅ Pass if… |
|:-:|------|--------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Power** | Switch on the power. | Within about a minute, a Wi-Fi network named after the D22's IMEI appears on your phone. |
| 2 | **Online** | Open Traqcare → **Map View** and find the D22. | The vehicle shows as **online** (coloured, not grey). |
| 3 | **Location** | Put the D22 near a window or outside, with a view of the sky. | The vehicle shows the right location on the map. GPS may not work deep indoors. That's normal. |
| 4 | **Live video** | Click the vehicle, then the **camera icon**. | You see live video from **every camera** (road and driver). |
| 5 | **Ignition** | In a vehicle, turn the ignition off and back on. (On a desk, disconnect and reconnect the **yellow** wire.) | The ignition status in Traqcare changes from off to on. |
| 6 | **SOS button** | Press the **SOS button** on the D22. | An **SOS** alarm appears in Traqcare. To get a notification, set up an SOS alert first: see [Creating Alerts](https://docs.traqcare.com/docs/alerts/creating-alerts). |
| 7 | **AI alarms** (AI versions only) | Follow [Test the AI alarms](#test-the-ai-alarms) below. | The D22 speaks a warning and the alarm appears in Traqcare. |

If a test fails, see [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md).

## Test the AI alarms

AI alarms normally only work while the vehicle is **driving**. To test them while parked or on a desk, switch the D22 to **Demo mode**. It then behaves as if the vehicle is driving at 60 km/h.

1. Connect your phone to the D22's Wi-Fi and log in with Traqcam Connect (password `111111`).
2. Go to **AI → Mode Settings**, set **Mode** to **demo mode** and tap **Save**. See [AI Features → Normal and Demo mode](./ai-features.mdx#3-mode-settings--normal-and-demo).
3. Sit in front of the **driver-facing camera** and try:

| Do this | Expected alarm |
|---------|----------------|
| Close your eyes for a few seconds | Fatigue driving (eyes closed) |
| Yawn | Fatigue driving (yawn) |
| Hold a phone to your ear | Phone |
| Look away to the side for a few seconds | Distracted driving |
| Cover the driver camera with your hand | Equipment occlusion (camera blocked) |

4. Each alarm should play a **voice warning** from the D22 and appear in Traqcare.

:::danger Switch back to Normal mode
When you finish testing, go back to **Mode Settings**, set **normal mode** and tap **Save**. If you leave Demo mode on, the D22 gives **false alarms** even when the vehicle is parked.
:::
