# D22 — AI Features (ADAS & DMS)

:::info AI versions only
AI alarms are available only on a D22 ordered **with DMS + ADAS**.
:::

The D22 has two kinds of AI:

- **DMS (Driver Monitoring):** watches the **driver** for fatigue, phone use, smoking, distraction and more.
- **ADAS (Driver Assistance):** watches the **road** for lane departure, collision risks and pedestrians.

When an AI alarm happens, the D22 **warns the driver with a voice message** and **sends the alarm to Traqcare**.

## Open the AI settings

1. Connect your phone to the D22's Wi-Fi (name = IMEI, password `88888888`).
2. Open **Traqcam Connect**, tap **Settings** and log in with `111111`.
3. Under **Alarm Settings**, tap **AI**.

The AI menu has: **Channel Settings**, **Alarm Settings**, **Mode Settings**, Driver Infor, AI customID and File Upload. You only need the first three.

## 1. Channel Settings

This page (titled **AI built in software**) links each AI function to a camera. The **Algorithm Version** is detected automatically and can't be changed.

:::tip Usually already set
Channel settings are normally set at the factory. Only change them if a camera isn't giving any AI alarms or our support team asks you to.
:::

For each channel you can choose:

- **CH (channel):** which camera the AI uses. **Close** means AI is off for that channel.
- **Resolution:** the picture quality the AI uses (default **1080P**).
- **Direction:** which way that camera faces, **FRONT** or **BEHIND**.
- Other options, such as camera type and the left/right blind-spot (BSD) settings, can stay at their defaults.

Tap **Save**. The app shows **Save OK**.

## 2. Alarm Settings

This page lists every AI alarm. Tap an alarm to open its settings and turn it on or off.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/d22/app-ai-alarms-dms.jpg" alt="AI alarm list: DMS alarms followed by ADAS alarms" width={220} caption="DMS alarms" />
  <Shot src="/img/d22/app-ai-alarms-adas.jpg" alt="AI alarm list: end of the DMS alarms and the ADAS alarms" width={220} caption="ADAS alarms" />
</Shots>

**DMS: driver alarms**

| Alarm | When it triggers |
|-------|------------------|
| Close the eyes (fatigue driving) | The driver's eyes stay closed |
| Yawn (fatigue driving) | The driver yawns |
| Phone | The driver holds a phone to their ear |
| Smoke | The driver is smoking |
| Cast about (distracted driving) | The driver looks away from the road for too long |
| Driver abnormal | No driver is detected in the seat |
| Safety belt | The driver isn't wearing a seat belt |
| Equipment occlusion | The driver camera is covered or blocked |
| Infrared blocking | The driver wears glasses that block the camera's infrared light |
| Identification | Driver identification |
| Drink water | The driver is drinking while driving |

**ADAS: road alarms**

| Alarm | When it triggers |
|-------|------------------|
| Lane departure warning | The vehicle drifts out of its lane without signalling |
| Forward collision | Risk of hitting the vehicle ahead |
| Pedestrian collision | Risk of hitting a pedestrian or cyclist ahead |
| Headway monitoring warning | Following the vehicle ahead too closely |
| Ahead car is starting | The vehicle ahead has moved off while you are still stopped |

Tap **Save** after making changes.

## 3. Mode Settings — Normal and Demo

| Mode | Use it for |
|------|-----------|
| **normal mode** | Real driving. AI alarms only trigger while the vehicle is moving. **Use this in vehicles.** |
| **demo mode** | Testing in an office or a parked vehicle. The D22 acts as if the vehicle is driving at 60 km/h, so the alarms can trigger. |

To change mode: **Mode Settings → Mode** → choose **normal mode** or **demo mode** → **Save**.

:::danger Don't leave Demo mode on
Demo mode is only for testing. Switch back to **normal mode** before the vehicle goes into service, or the D22 gives false alarms while parked.
:::

:::warning
AI alarms help the driver but **can't replace the driver's attention**. Accuracy depends on light, weather and road conditions, and is not 100 %.
:::

To try the alarms step by step, see [Test Your D22 → Test the AI alarms](./test-device#test-the-ai-alarms).
