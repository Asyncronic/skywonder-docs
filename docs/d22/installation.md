# D22 — Installation

:::tip Testing before installing?
You can set up and test the D22 on a desk first. Do steps 1 and 2 below with a 12 V power supply, then go to [Make It Online on Traqcare](./make-online). Mount it in the vehicle once everything works.
:::

## 1. Insert the SIM card and memory card

Do this **with the power off**.

1. **SIM card:** use a SIM with an active **mobile data plan**. Turn off the SIM PIN first (put the SIM in a phone and disable "SIM PIN").
2. **TF (microSD) card:** use a good-quality card of **Class 10 or faster**, 16 GB or larger. The D22 records video to this card.

## 2. Connect the power

The D22 runs on **10–36 V DC**, which covers both 12 V and 24 V vehicles.

| Wire | In the vehicle | On a desk (12 V power supply) |
|------|----------------|-------------------------------|
| **Red** | Battery **+** (constant power) | **+** |
| **Black** | Ground / chassis **−** | **−** |
| **Yellow** | **ACC**: a wire that has power only when the ignition is on | **+** (together with red) |

On a desk, connect **yellow to + as well**. This tells the D22 the "ignition" is on, so it stays fully awake. If the yellow wire has no power, the D22 goes into low-power standby.

:::warning
Double-check red and black before you switch on. Connecting them the wrong way round can damage the device.
:::

When power is on, the D22 starts in about 10 seconds and turns on its Wi-Fi automatically.

## 3. Mount the D22 in the vehicle

Mount the D22 at the **top of the windshield**, near the centre. Make sure that:

- It does **not block the driver's view**.
- It is **level**, not tilted to one side.
- The road-facing camera sees the road ahead clearly. The horizon should be near the middle of its picture.
- The driver-facing camera sees the **driver's face clearly** (important for the AI features). Check this in the live video after setup.
- It is **not** in front of or on top of an airbag.

Run the cables neatly along the trim so they don't hang near the driver or the pedals.
