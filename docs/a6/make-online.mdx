# A6 — Make It Online on Traqcare

By the end of this page your A6 is **online on Traqcare** and you can watch its live video.

**You will need:**

- The A6, with the SIM card and memory card inserted and power connected (see [Installation](./installation)).
- A smartphone with the free **Cloud Spirit** app, from **Google Play** (Android) or the **App Store** (iPhone).
- A login at [track.traqcare.com](https://track.traqcare.com). **Coins** to activate the device are helpful but not required now: you can activate later (see Step 6).

## The steps at a glance

| Step | What you do | Where |
|:----:|-------------|-------|
| 1 | Turn on the A6's Wi-Fi and connect your phone | A6 + phone |
| 2 | Open Cloud Spirit and check the live video | Cloud Spirit |
| 3 | Point the A6 at Traqcare | Cloud Spirit |
| 4 | Turn on parking recording | Cloud Spirit |
| 5 | Find your Device ID | Cloud Spirit |
| 6 | Add the A6 to your account | Traqcare |
| 7 | Turn on the sensors and set up the cameras | Traqcare |
| 8 | Check that it's online and watch live video | Traqcare |

## Step 1 — Turn on the A6's Wi-Fi and connect your phone

1. With the A6 switched on, **press the power button (⏻) twice quickly**. The A6 makes a sound to confirm its Wi-Fi is on.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/a6/a6-buttons.png" alt="A6 button panel with the power button at the top and the SOS button below it" width={480} caption="Press the power button (⏻) twice quickly" />
</Shots>

2. On your phone, open **Settings → Wi-Fi**.
3. Connect to the network named **A6-xxxx** (for example `A6_0d7a`).
4. Enter the password **`12345678`**.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/a6/phone-wifi.jpg" alt="Phone Wi-Fi list connected to A6_0d7a" width={220} caption="Connect to A6-xxxx, password 12345678" />
</Shots>

:::note "No internet connection" is normal
Your phone may say the A6's Wi-Fi has **no internet**. That's expected, so stay connected. If your phone keeps switching back to mobile data or another Wi-Fi, turn off mobile data until you finish Step 5.
:::

## Step 2 — Open Cloud Spirit and check the live video

Open **Cloud Spirit**. It finds the A6 automatically, and the **Live Video** tab shows the camera picture.

## Step 3 — Point the A6 at Traqcare

1. Tap the **settings (gear) icon** at the top right.
2. Tap **JT808**.
3. Change these two fields:

| Field on the screen | Meaning | Enter |
|---------------------|---------|-------|
| **主IP** | Main IP | `d4.traqcare.com` |
| **主端口** | Main port | `5101` |

4. Leave the other fields (IP2, Port2 and the rest) as they are.
5. Tap **Save**.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/a6/app-home.jpg" alt="Cloud Spirit home screen with the settings gear highlighted at the top right" width={240} caption="1. Tap the gear" />
  <Shot src="/img/a6/app-settings.jpg" alt="Cloud Spirit Settings with JT808 highlighted in red" width={240} caption="2. Tap JT808 (in red)" />
  <Shot src="/img/a6/app-jt808.jpg" alt="JT808 page with Main IP d4.traqcare.com, Main port 5101 and the Save button highlighted in red" width={240} caption="3. Change Main IP (主IP) and Main port (主端口), then tap Save" />
</Shots>

## Step 4 — Turn on parking recording

1. Go back to **Settings** and tap **General**.
2. Tap **TLR** (time-lapse recording), choose **24H** and tap **Confirm**.
3. Scroll down to **Parking Surveillance** and switch **Sentry Mode** on.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/a6/app-settings-general.jpg" alt="Settings with General highlighted" width={200} caption="1. General" />
  <Shot src="/img/a6/app-tlr.jpg" alt="TLR options Off, 12H, 24H with 24H selected" width={200} caption="2. TLR: 24H" />
  <Shot src="/img/a6/app-sentry-mode.jpg" alt="General settings with Sentry Mode switched on" width={200} caption="3. Sentry Mode on" />
</Shots>

These keep recording while the vehicle is parked, as long as the A6 has power (see [Installation](./installation#3-connect-the-power)).

## Step 5 — Find your Device ID

In Cloud Spirit, go to **Settings → JT808** and look for **Phone Number**. **This number is the A6's Device ID.** Write it down.

:::warning Remove the leading 0
If the Device ID starts with **0**, leave out that first 0 everywhere in Traqcare. Traqcare never uses the leading 0.

| Phone Number in Cloud Spirit | Device ID for Traqcare |
|------------------------------|------------------------|
| `05848956245` | `5848956245` |
:::

You can now disconnect your phone from the A6's Wi-Fi.

## Step 6 — Add the A6 to your Traqcare account

1. Log in at [track.traqcare.com](https://track.traqcare.com).
2. Go to **Settings** (gear icon) → **Devices** → **Add Device**.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/a6/traqcare-devices.png" alt="Traqcare Settings, Devices tab with the Add Device button highlighted" width={720} />
</Shots>

3. Fill in the **Device Details** tab:

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Client** | The account that will own the A6 |
| **IMEI / Unique ID** | The **Device ID** from Step 5 (no leading 0) |
| **Protocol / Port** | **JT808 (5101)** |
| **Model Name** | `A6` |
| **Device Type** | **Dashcam** |
| **Device Timezone** | The vehicle's time zone |
| **Object Name** | A name for the vehicle, anything you like |
| **Object Type** | **Car** (or the right vehicle type) |
| **Subscription Term** | Choose a plan if you see one. Coins are used to activate the device. **If the list is empty, leave it blank**: see the note below. |

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/a6/traqcare-add-device.png" alt="Device Details tab with protocol JT808 (5101), model A6 and Device Type Dashcam highlighted in red" width={620} caption="Device Type must be Dashcam" />
</Shots>

:::info No plans in "Subscription Term"? You can activate later
Each plan in the list shows how many coins you have, for example *1 Year (Available Coins: 20)*. **Most new accounts have no coins yet, so the list is empty.** That's fine:

1. Leave **Subscription Term** blank and finish adding the device (Step 7). The A6 is saved in your account but **not activated yet**.
2. **Get coins:** ask your supplier (Skywonder or the reseller you bought the A6 from) to add coins to your account.
3. **Activate:** go to **Settings → Devices**, find your A6 and click **Activate** in its row. Choose a plan and confirm.

If you don't see an **Activate** link, your supplier can activate the device for you.

The A6 needs to be **activated** before it shows up as online and before you can use it on Traqcare.
:::

## Step 7 — Turn on the sensors and set up the cameras

1. Open the **Device Sensors** tab and switch these **on**:

| Sensor | Parameter | Reading Type |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| **IGNITION** | `acc` | Direct |
| **POWER** | `power` | Direct |
| **CAMERA** | — | — |
| **VIDEO CAMERA** | — | — |

Leave the other sensors off.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/a6/traqcare-sensors-1.png" alt="Device Sensors tab with IGNITION (acc) and POWER (power) switched on" width={420} caption="IGNITION and POWER" />
  <Shot src="/img/a6/traqcare-sensors-2.png" alt="Device Sensors tab with CAMERA and VIDEO CAMERA switched on" width={420} caption="CAMERA and VIDEO CAMERA" />
</Shots>

2. A **Camera Settings** tab appears. Open it and fill in:

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Number of Channels** | **2** (front and rear/cabin camera) |
| **DeviceId** | The **same Device ID** you entered in Step 6 (no leading 0) |
| **Video Platform** | **Traqcare** |

3. Click **Submit**. If you chose a plan in Step 6, the coins are used and the A6 is activated. If you didn't, activate it later (see the note in Step 6).

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/a6/traqcare-camera-settings.png" alt="Camera Settings tab with 2 channels, device ID and video platform Traqcare" width={620} />
</Shots>

## Step 8 — Check that it's online and watch live video

1. Open the **Map View** in Traqcare.
2. Find your A6 in the vehicle list. Within a few minutes it turns **online** (coloured instead of grey).
3. Click the **camera icon** next to the vehicle to open live video.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/a6/traqcare-map-camera.png" alt="Traqcare Map View with the camera icon next to a vehicle highlighted" width={720} caption="Click the camera icon next to the vehicle" />
</Shots>

If you see live video from both cameras, **your A6 is set up correctly**. 🎉

Not online after 5 minutes? See [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting#a6-is-not-online-on-traqcare).
