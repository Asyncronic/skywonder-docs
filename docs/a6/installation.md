# A6 — Installation

The **A6** is a 4G dashcam with **2 cameras**: a built-in front 2K camera and a 720P rear camera on a cable. You set it up with the free **Cloud Spirit** app on your phone, then add it to Traqcare.

:::tip Testing before installing?
You can set up and test the A6 on a desk first. Do steps 1 to 3 below, then go to [Make It Online on Traqcare](./make-online.mdx). Install it in the vehicle once everything works.
:::

## 1. Insert the SIM card and memory card

Do this **with the power off**.

1. **SIM card:** use a SIM with an active **mobile data plan**. Turn off the SIM PIN first (put the SIM in a phone and disable "SIM PIN").
2. **TF (microSD) card:** use a good-quality card of **Class 10 or faster**. The A6 records video to this card.

## 2. Connect the rear camera

Plug the rear camera cable into the A6. Mount the rear camera where you want to record: on the rear window, or inside facing the cabin.

## 3. Connect the power

The A6 runs on **12 V to 24 V**. There are two ways to power it:

| Option | How | Best for |
|--------|-----|----------|
| **Cigarette-lighter plug** | Plug it into the vehicle's 12 V socket | Quick setup and testing |
| **Hardwire kit** | Connect the wires to the vehicle's fuse box (see below) | Permanent installation, and recording while parked (**Sentry Mode**) |

**Hardwire kit wires:**

| Wire | Connect to |
|------|-----------|
| **Red** | Constant power **+** (has power even when the ignition is off) |
| **Black** | Ground / chassis **−** |
| **Yellow** | **ACC**: a wire that has power only when the ignition is on |

:::warning
Double-check red and black before you switch on. Connecting them the wrong way round can damage the device.
:::

:::info Recording while parked needs the hardwire kit
With the hardwire kit, the A6 stays connected to the battery when the ignition is off and goes into a deep sleep that uses almost no power (3 mA or less), so it won't drain the battery. **Sentry Mode** and **time-lapse recording** need this. With the cigarette-lighter plug, most vehicles cut the power when the ignition is off.
:::

## 4. Mount the A6 in the vehicle

Mount the A6 at the **top of the windshield**, near the centre, behind the rear-view mirror if possible. Make sure that:

- It does **not block the driver's view**.
- The front camera sees the road ahead clearly.
- The rear or cabin camera points where you want to record.
- It is **not** in front of or on top of an airbag.

Run the cable neatly along the trim so it doesn't hang near the driver or the pedals.
