# A6 — Test Your A6

Use this checklist after [Make It Online on Traqcare](./make-online.mdx). It takes about **10 minutes**.

| # | Test | How to do it | ✅ Pass if… |
|:-:|------|--------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Power** | Switch on the A6. | The A6 switches on. |
| 2 | **Wi-Fi** | Press the power button (⏻) twice quickly. | You hear a sound and a Wi-Fi network named **A6-xxxx** appears on your phone. |
| 3 | **App video** | Connect to the A6's Wi-Fi and open **Cloud Spirit**. | The **Live Video** tab shows the camera picture. |
| 4 | **Online** | Disconnect your phone from the A6's Wi-Fi. Open Traqcare → **Map View** and find the A6. | The vehicle shows as **online** (coloured, not grey). |
| 5 | **Location** | Put the A6 near a window or outside. | The vehicle shows the right location on the map. GPS may not work deep indoors. That's normal. |
| 6 | **Live video** | Click the **camera icon** next to the vehicle in Traqcare. | You see live video from **both cameras**. |
| 7 | **Recordings** | In Cloud Spirit, open the **Playback** tab. | Recorded videos are listed and play. |
| 8 | **SOS button** | Press the **SOS** button. | An **SOS** alarm appears in Traqcare. To get a notification, set up an SOS alert first: see [Creating Alerts](https://docs.traqcare.com/docs/alerts/creating-alerts). |
| 9 | **Parking recording** (hardwire kit only) | Turn the ignition off and leave the vehicle parked. | The A6 keeps recording in **Sentry Mode** / time-lapse. |

If a test fails, see [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md).
