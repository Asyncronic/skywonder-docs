# D12 — Driving Assistance Display (up to 5 cameras)

The **D12** is a **10.1-inch touchscreen** that records up to **5 cameras**, sends its live location and video to **Traqcare** over 4G, and uses AI to warn the driver about blind spots, fatigue, phone use and other risks. Everything is set up **on the screen itself**, so you don't need an app.

## What it does

| Feature | What you get |
|---------|--------------|
| **Video** | Up to **5 cameras** (1080p), recorded with sound to an SD card or an optional mSATA hard drive. Watch live and recorded video in Traqcare. |
| **Screen** | Built-in 10.1-inch touchscreen showing all cameras. All settings are made on the screen. |
| **Tracking** | GPS / BeiDou location sent to Traqcare over 4G. |
| **AI** | **DMS** watches the driver (fatigue, phone, smoking, distraction, seat belt). **ADAS** watches the road. **BSD** (blind-spot detection) warns about people and vehicles around the vehicle. |
| **Alarms** | SOS emergency button input, harsh driving and collision alarms. |
| **Voice** | Built-in speaker and microphone for two-way talk with the platform. |
