# D12 — Test Your D12

Use this checklist after [Make It Online on Traqcare](./make-online.mdx). It takes about **10 minutes**. You can do it on a desk with a 12 V power supply or in the vehicle.

| # | Test | How to do it | ✅ Pass if… |
|:-:|------|--------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Cameras** | Look at the D12 screen. | Every connected camera (CH1–CH5) shows a picture. |
| 2 | **Status icons** | Look at the icons at the top right of the screen. | **4G**, **platform** and **SD card** icons are bright. **GPS** is bright outdoors (it can stay grey indoors). See [status icons](./specifications.md#screen-status-icons). |
| 3 | **Online** | Open Traqcare → **Map View** and find the D12. | The vehicle shows as **online** (coloured, not grey). |
| 4 | **Location** | Put the GPS antenna near a window or outside. | The vehicle shows the right location on the map. |
| 5 | **Live video** | Click the vehicle in Traqcare, then the **camera icon**. | You see live video from **every camera**. |
| 6 | **Recording on the D12** | On the D12, tap **Settings → VideoPlay**, choose **SD** (or **MSATA**) and open **Record**. | Recorded videos are listed and play. |
| 7 | **Video playback in Traqcare** | In Traqcare, open **Video Playback** for the vehicle (see below) and search today's date. | Recordings from the D12 are found and play. |
| 8 | **Ignition** | In a vehicle, turn the ignition off and on. (On a desk, disconnect and reconnect the **ACC** wire.) | The ignition status in Traqcare changes. With ACC off, the D12 turns off after a delay. |
| 9 | **SOS button** (if connected) | Press the SOS button. | An **SOS** alarm appears in Traqcare. To get a notification, set up an SOS alert first: see [Creating Alerts](https://docs.traqcare.com/docs/alerts/creating-alerts). |
| 10 | **AI alarms** | Follow [Test the AI alarms](#test-the-ai-alarms) below. | The D12 speaks a warning and the alarm appears in Traqcare. |

If a test fails, see [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md).

## Watch recordings

**On the D12 screen:** tap **Settings → VideoPlay**. Choose where the videos are stored, **SD** or **MSATA**, then open **Record** (videos) or **Capture** (photos).

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/d12/d12-videoplay.jpg" alt="VideoPlay page with SD or MSATA selection and Capture / Record options" width={460} caption="VideoPlay: choose SD or MSATA" />
</Shots>

**In Traqcare:**

1. In **Map View**, open the vehicle's menu in the object list and choose **Video Playback**.
2. Choose the **date range** and click **Search Videos**.
3. The **Device** tab lists the recordings on the D12. Click one to play or download it.

## Test the AI alarms

AI alarms only work above **30 km/h**. To test them while parked or on a desk, give the D12 a **simulated speed** of 60 km/h:

1. On the D12, tap **Settings → System**.
2. Set **Simulated speed** to **`60`** and tap **Confirm**. The screen shows **Simulation 60**.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/d12/d12-system-simulated-speed.jpg" alt="System page with Simulated speed set to 60" width={460} caption="System → Simulated speed: 60" />
</Shots>

3. Sit in front of the **driver camera (CH4)** and try:

| Do this | Expected alarm |
|---------|----------------|
| Close your eyes for 2–3 seconds | Fatigue: *"Please take a break"* |
| Yawn (open your mouth wide) | Fatigue |
| Hold a phone to your ear above your chin and talk | Phone: *"Do not use phone"* |
| Look to the side or down for 2–3 seconds | Distraction: *"Please concentrate on driving"* |
| Hold a cigarette (or a pen like one) to your mouth | Smoking: *"Please do not smoke"* |
| Sit without a seat belt | Seat belt: *"Please fasten your seatbelt"* |

4. To test a **blind-spot camera** (CH1, CH2, CH3 or CH5), walk into its view. The D12 should warn about a person in that blind spot.
5. Each alarm plays a **voice warning** from the D12 and appears in Traqcare.

:::danger Set the simulated speed back to 0
When you finish testing, go back to **Settings → System**, set **Simulated speed** to **`0`** and tap **Confirm**. While it's set, the D12 acts as if the vehicle is driving and gives **false alarms**. Restarting the D12 also switches it back to real GPS speed.
:::
