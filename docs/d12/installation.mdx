# D12 — Installation

:::tip Testing before installing?
You can set up and test the D12 on a desk first with a 12 V power supply. Do steps 1 to 4 below, then go to [Make It Online on Traqcare](./make-online.mdx). Install it in the vehicle once everything works.
:::

## 1. Insert the SIM card and SD card

Do this **with the power off**.

1. Use a screwdriver to open the small cover on the **top edge** of the D12.
2. Insert the **SIM card** and the **SD card** into the slots marked **SIM** and **SD**.
3. Close the cover again.

- **SIM card:** must have an active **mobile data plan**, with the SIM PIN turned off.
- **SD card:** good quality, Class 10 or faster, up to 512 GB.
- **mSATA hard drive (optional):** if you ordered one, it fits in the **MINI SATA** compartment on the back.

## 2. Connect the cameras and antennas

All connectors are on the **back** of the D12, labelled from left to right: **GPS · CH5 · CH4 · CH3 · CH2 · CH1 · USB · 4G**.

1. **Cameras:** plug each camera into its channel (**CH1 to CH5**) and screw the connector ring on firmly. Use the channel that matches what the camera is for:

| Channel | Camera |
|---------|--------|
| **CH1** | Front blind-spot (BSD) camera |
| **CH2** | Rear blind-spot (BSD) camera |
| **CH3** | Left blind-spot (BSD) camera |
| **CH4** | **Driver (DMS) camera**, facing the driver |
| **CH5** | Right blind-spot (BSD) camera |

2. **GPS antenna:** screw it onto the **GPS** connector (far left).
3. **4G antenna:** screw it onto the **4G** connector (far right).
4. **Power cable:** connect the power / video input cable (the one with the labelled wires) to the D12 and tighten the connector.

## 3. Connect the power

The D12 runs on **9–36 V DC**, which covers both 12 V and 24 V vehicles. The power wires are labelled.

| Wire label | In the vehicle | On a desk (12 V power supply) |
|------------|----------------|-------------------------------|
| **B+** | Battery **+** (constant power) | **+** |
| **ACC** | A wire that has power only when the ignition is on | **+** (together with B+) |
| **GND** | Ground / chassis **−** | **−** |

On a desk, connect **ACC to + as well**. This tells the D12 the "ignition" is on, so it stays on. When ACC has no power, the D12 turns off after a delay or goes to sleep.

Leave the other wires (turn signals, SOS, inputs, RS232) unconnected unless you are installing those accessories.

:::warning
Double-check **B+** and **GND** before you switch on. The D12 has reverse-polarity protection, but always connect carefully.
:::

## 4. Switch on

Switch on the power. The D12 starts and shows the **camera views** on the screen.

## 5. Install in the vehicle

- Mount the D12 with the metal bracket where the driver can see the screen **without it blocking the view of the road**.
- Mount the **driver (DMS) camera** so it sees the **driver's face clearly**.
- Mount the blind-spot cameras so each one covers its side of the vehicle.
- Do **not** install anything in front of or on top of an airbag.
- Run the cables neatly along the trim, away from the pedals and steering.
