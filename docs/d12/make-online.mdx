# D12 — Make It Online on Traqcare

By the end of this page your D12 is **online on Traqcare** and you can watch live video from its cameras.

**You will need:**

- The D12 installed and switched on, with SIM card, SD card, cameras and antennas connected (see [Installation](./installation.mdx)).
- The **IMEI number** from the label on the back of the D12 (next to **MINI SATA**).
- A login at [track.traqcare.com](https://track.traqcare.com). **Coins** to activate the device are helpful but not required now: you can activate later (see Step 3).

## The steps at a glance

| Step | What you do | Where |
|:----:|-------------|-------|
| 1 | Find your Device ID | D12 label |
| 2 | Set the Traqcare server | D12 screen |
| 3 | Add the D12 to your account | Traqcare |
| 4 | Turn on the camera sensors | Traqcare |
| 5 | Set up the cameras | Traqcare |
| 6 | Check that it's online and watch live video | Traqcare |

## Step 1 — Find your Device ID

The D12's Device ID is the **last 12 digits of its IMEI**. The IMEI is printed on the label on the back of the D12.

| IMEI on the label | Device ID (last 12 digits) |
|-------------------|----------------------------|
| `862813086562629` | `813086562629` |

:::warning No leading 0
If the Device ID starts with **0**, leave out that first 0 everywhere in Traqcare. Traqcare never uses the leading 0.
:::

## Step 2 — Set the Traqcare server on the D12

1. On the D12 screen, tap the **Settings** button (the yellow **gear** icon at the bottom).
2. If the D12 asks for a password, enter **`00000000`** (eight 0s).
3. Tap **Platform**, then **Server**.
4. Set **Center 1**:

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Server address of Center 1** | `d4.traqcare.com` |
| **Port number of Center 1** | `5101` |
| **Center 1 Protocol type** | `2013` |

5. Leave the **Center 2** settings as they are.
6. Tap **Confirm** to save.

## Step 3 — Add the D12 to your Traqcare account

1. Log in at [track.traqcare.com](https://track.traqcare.com).
2. Go to **Settings** (gear icon) → **Devices** → **Add Device**.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/d12/traqcare-devices.png" alt="Traqcare Settings, Devices tab with the Add Device button highlighted" width={720} />
</Shots>

3. Fill in the **Device Details** tab:

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Client** | The account that will own the D12 |
| **IMEI / Unique ID** | The **Device ID** from Step 1 (last 12 digits of the IMEI, no leading 0) |
| **Protocol / Port** | **JT808 (5101)** |
| **Model Name** | `D12` |
| **Device Type** | **Wired** |
| **Device Timezone** | The vehicle's time zone |
| **Object Name** | A name for the vehicle, anything you like |
| **Object Type** | **Car** (or the right vehicle type, e.g. truck or bus) |
| **Subscription Term** | Choose a plan if you see one. Coins are used to activate the device. **If the list is empty, leave it blank**: see the note below. |

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/d12/traqcare-add-device.png" alt="Add Device form, Device Details tab, with protocol JT808 (5101) and model D12" width={620} />
</Shots>

:::info No plans in "Subscription Term"? You can activate later
Each plan in the list shows how many coins you have, for example *1 Year (Available Coins: 20)*. **Most new accounts have no coins yet, so the list is empty.** That's fine:

1. Leave **Subscription Term** blank and finish adding the device (Steps 4 and 5). The D12 is saved in your account but **not activated yet**.
2. **Get coins:** ask your supplier (Skywonder or the reseller you bought the D12 from) to add coins to your account.
3. **Activate:** go to **Settings → Devices**, find your D12 and click **Activate** in its row. Choose a plan and confirm.

If you don't see an **Activate** link, your supplier can activate the device for you.

The D12 needs to be **activated** before it shows up as online and before you can use it on Traqcare.
:::

## Step 4 — Turn on the camera sensors

Open the **Device Sensors** tab and switch these **on**:

| Sensor | Parameter | Reading Type |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| **IGNITION** | `acc` | Direct |
| **POWER** | `power` | Direct |
| **CAMERA** | — | — |
| **VIDEO CAMERA** | — | — |

Leave the other sensors off.

## Step 5 — Set up the cameras

When **VIDEO CAMERA** is on, a **Camera Settings** tab appears. Open it and fill in:

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Number of Channels** | Match the number of cameras you connected. If that number isn't in the list, pick the next bigger one: **1** camera → `1` · **2** → `2` · **3–4** → `4` · **5** → `6` |
| **DeviceId** | The **same Device ID** you entered in Step 3 (no leading 0) |
| **Video Platform** | **Traqcare** |

Click **Submit**. If you chose a plan in Step 3, the coins are used and the D12 is activated. If you didn't, activate it later (see the note in Step 3).

## Step 6 — Check that it's online and watch live video

1. On the D12 screen, the **platform icon** at the top right turns **bright** when it connects to Traqcare (see [status icons](./specifications.md#screen-status-icons)).
2. In Traqcare, open the **Map View** and find your D12 in the vehicle list. It shows as **online** (coloured instead of grey).
3. Click the vehicle, then click the **camera icon** to open live video.

You should see a live picture from **every camera** you connected (CH1 to CH5). If you do, **your D12 is set up correctly**. 🎉

  <Shot src="/img/d12/traqcare-live-video.png" alt="Traqcare live video with CH1 to CH5 showing pictures" width={560} caption="Live video in Traqcare: CH1 to CH5" />

Not online after 5 minutes? See [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md#d12-is-not-online-on-traqcare).
