# H8 — Bluetooth Fuel Sensor Setup

This guide takes you from a new Bluetooth fuel sensor to **live fuel levels on Traqcare**. Follow the steps in order.

**You will need:**

- Your **H8 GPS tracker**, installed in the vehicle and online on Traqcare.
- The **Bluetooth fuel sensor** (model YSBLE-B).
- A **smartphone** with Bluetooth and Location (GPS) turned on.
- Your **fuel tank capacity** in litres.
- A login to [track.traqcare.com](https://track.traqcare.com).

## The steps at a glance

| Step | What you do | Where |
|:----:|-------------|-------|
| 1 | Check whether the sensor probe was cut | On the sensor |
| 2 | Install the sensor app | Phone |
| 3 | Connect the phone to the sensor | Sensor app |
| 4 | Calibrate the sensor | Sensor app |
| 5 | Install the sensor and check the reading | Sensor app |
| 6 | Link the sensor to the H8 | Traqcare or SMS |
| 7 | Turn on the fuel sensor in Traqcare | Traqcare |
| 8 | Fill in the fuel calibration form | Traqcare |
| 9 | Check the live fuel level | Traqcare |

## Step 1 — Was the sensor probe cut?

The sensor probe (the long metal rod) is often cut so that it fits the depth of the tank. This decides which steps you need:

| Your situation | What to do |
|----------------|------------|
| **Probe NOT cut** (original length) | The sensor is already calibrated at the factory (range **0–65535**). Write down the MAC address from the sensor's label, then **go straight to [Step 6](#step-6--link-the-sensor-to-the-h8)**. |
| **Probe cut** to fit the tank | Cutting changes the sensor's "empty" and "full" points, so it **must be calibrated again**. Do **Steps 2 to 9**. |

The MAC address is printed on the top of the sensor, for example `F7:DA:B8:E9:2B:6E`. You will need it in Step 3 and Step 6.

**Before you open the app**, turn on **Bluetooth** and **Location** on your phone. The app uses Location to find nearby Bluetooth sensors and will not find the sensor without it. When the app asks for Bluetooth or Location permission, tap **Allow**.

## Step 3 — Connect the phone to the sensor

1. Open the app and tap **Device list**.
2. Tap **YSBLE-B — sensor device**.
3. The app searches for nearby sensors. Find the one whose **MAC address matches the label on your sensor**, then tap **connect**.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/h8/app-find-sensor.png" alt="Device list, then YSBLE-B sensor device, then the nearby sensor list with the MAC address highlighted" width={720} caption="Device list → YSBLE-B → connect to the sensor with your MAC address." />
</Shots>

4. **The first time you connect**, the app asks you to create a **6-digit password** (numbers only). This password protects the sensor's settings.

:::warning Write the password down
You will need this password every time you connect to the sensor. To change or remove it later, open the **password** menu and enter the old password.
:::

After connecting, you will see the sensor menu: **reading**, **set up**, **password** and **calibration**.

### Sensor settings (leave as they are)

The **set up** menu shows two values. Both are set at the factory and **should not be changed**:

| Setting | Factory value | What it means |
|---------|:-------------:|---------------|
| Highest value | `65535` | The sensor's top reading (a full tank). |
| Damping coefficient | `6` | Smooths out fuel moving around in the tank while driving. A higher number gives steadier readings but reacts more slowly. |

## Step 4 — Calibrate the sensor

Only needed if the probe was **cut** (see Step 1). Open the **calibration** menu. There are two ways to calibrate. **Manual calibration is more accurate** and is the one we recommend.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/h8/app-calibration.png" alt="Calibration page with the Remove fuel for calibration switch, Mark empty, Marked full and Confirm calibration buttons" width={260} caption="The calibration page." />
</Shots>

### Method A — Manual calibration (recommended)

1. Turn **OFF** the **Remove fuel for calibration** switch.
2. **Set the empty point:** hold the sensor **upright in the air** (dry, not in fuel). Wait until the status on the right shows **stable**, then tap **Mark empty**. Wait for the success message.
3. **Set the full point:** stand the sensor upright in a tube or container **filled with the same fuel the vehicle uses**. The fuel must cover the **small vent hole at the top of the probe**. Wait until the status shows **stable**, then tap **Marked full**.

When both points are saved, calibration is complete.

### Method B — Automatic calibration

1. Turn **ON** the **Remove fuel for calibration** switch.
2. Hold the sensor **upright in the air**. Wait until the status shows **stable**.
3. Tap **Confirm calibration**. Calibration is done when the app shows **Calibration complete**.

## Step 5 — Install the sensor and check the reading

1. Install the sensor in the fuel tank.
2. In the app, open the **reading** menu.
3. The page shows the sensor name, MAC address, **analog quantity**, temperature, raw data, and a fuel level picture on the right.
4. Check that the level matches the fuel in the tank. When fuel is added or used, the reading should change. If it does, the sensor is working correctly.

## Step 6 — Link the sensor to the H8

Send this command to the H8 so it knows which sensor to read:

```text
BLELIST,<MAC>;0;1#
```

Replace `<MAC>` with your sensor's MAC address, written **without the colons** and in **capital letters**. Keep `;0;1#` exactly as it is.

| MAC on the sensor label | Command to send |
|-------------------------|-----------------|
| `CE:26:1F:75:B8:47` | `BLELIST,CE261F75B847;0;1#` |
| `F7:DA:B8:E9:2B:6E` | `BLELIST,F7DAB8E92B6E;0;1#` |

You can send the command in either of these ways:

**Option A — From Traqcare** (the H8 must be online)

1. Log in at [track.traqcare.com](https://track.traqcare.com) and select the vehicle.
2. Open **Send GPRS Command** from the vehicle's action menu.
3. Choose a **custom command**, paste your `BLELIST` command and send it.
4. The device's reply appears in the command history.

**Option B — By SMS**

Send the `BLELIST` command as a text message to the **phone number of the SIM card inside the H8**. The SIM must be able to receive SMS.

## Step 7 — Turn on the fuel sensor in Traqcare

**First, find which channel carries the fuel reading.** It is usually `io270`, `ADC1` or `ADC2`.

1. On the Traqcare map, click your vehicle and open the **Parameters** tab.
2. Look for `io270`, `ADC1` or `ADC2`. The one that shows a number (and changes when the fuel level changes) is your fuel channel. In the example below it is **io270**.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/h8/traqcare-parameters.png" alt="Parameters tab showing io270 with value 3349" width={560} />
</Shots>

**Then turn on the fuel sensor:**

1. Go to **Settings → Devices**, find your H8 and click **Modify**.
2. Open the **Device Sensors** tab.
3. Switch on **FUEL**.
4. In **Parameter**, choose the channel you found above (`io270`, `ADC1` or `ADC2`).
5. Set **Reading Type** to **Direct**.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/h8/traqcare-device-sensors.png" alt="Device Sensors tab with FUEL switched on and parameter io270" width={720} />
</Shots>

:::tip Adding a new H8?
If the H8 is not on Traqcare yet, add it first under **Settings → Devices → Add Device** and select the **JT808** protocol. See [Adding a Device](https://docs.traqcare.com/docs/device-management/adding-device).
:::

## Step 8 — Fill in the fuel calibration form

The **Fuel Calibration** tab appears once the fuel sensor is switched on. Open it and enter:

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Sensor Output Unit | **Voltage** |
| Conversion Factor | **1.0** |
| Calibration Type | **Automatic** |
| Empty Tank Reading | **0** |
| Half Tank Reading | Leave empty |
| Full Tank Reading | **65535** |
| Tank Capacity | Your tank size in **litres** (for example `650`) |

Click **Submit**.

<Shots>
  <Shot src="/img/h8/traqcare-fuel-calibration.png" alt="Fuel Calibration form with Empty 0, Full 65535 and Tank Capacity 650" width={720} />
</Shots>

:::note
Make sure the **Tank Capacity** is correct. Traqcare uses it to turn the sensor reading into litres, so a wrong value gives wrong fuel figures.
:::

## Step 9 — Check the live fuel level

That's it. Open the vehicle on the Traqcare map: the fuel level now updates in real time. Fuel history is available in the fuel chart and fuel reports — see [Fuel Management](https://docs.traqcare.com/docs/fuel-management) in the Traqcare platform docs.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
|---------|---------------|
| The app does not find the sensor | Bluetooth **and** Location are on, the app has Location permission, and the phone is close to the sensor. Make sure no other phone is connected to the sensor. |
| No fuel value in Traqcare | The H8 is online; the `BLELIST` command used the correct MAC (no colons, capital letters); in **Parameters**, the fuel channel shows a value; the same channel is selected in **Device Sensors**. |
| Fuel level is wrong | If the probe was cut, calibrate it again (Step 4). Check that Empty is `0`, Full is `65535` and the tank capacity is correct (Step 8). |
| Reading jumps up and down | Normal while driving, as fuel moves in the tank. Leave the damping coefficient at the factory value `6`. |
| Forgot the sensor password | Contact Skywonder support. |

Still stuck? Raise a ticket at [track.traqcare.com/ticket](https://track.traqcare.com/ticket) with your H8 IMEI and the sensor's MAC address.
