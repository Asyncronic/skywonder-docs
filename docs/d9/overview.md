# D9 — Driving Assistance Equipment

The **D9** is a comprehensive automotive active-safety device that combines satellite positioning, dual-channel video recording, and AI-powered driver assistance into one integrated unit. It is purpose-built for fleet monitoring on online car-hailing, taxi, and logistics vehicles.

Under the hood it runs deep-learning ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance System), DMS (Driver Monitoring System), and LCA (Lane Change Assist) algorithms, and speaks the Chinese national-standard transport protocols — **JT/T808**, **JT/T905**, and **JT/T1078** — so it plugs directly into the Traqcare platform.

## What It Does

| Capability | Summary |
|------------|---------|
| **ADAS** | Forward collision warning, pedestrian collision warning, lane departure, virtual bumper, start-up reminder. |
| **DMS** | Fatigue / drowsiness, distraction, phone use, smoking, seatbelt detection. |
| **Video** | Dual 1080p cameras (forward + cabin), H.265/H.264 local recording to TF card. |
| **Connectivity** | 4G LTE, Wi-Fi, GPS/GNSS. |
| **Voice** | Two-way voice intercom and monitoring via the platform. |
| **Alarms** | SOS button, speeding, fatigue driving, parking timeout, emergency alarm. |
| **Remote** | Real-time streaming, playback, remote photo capture, OTA firmware upgrade. |

## When to Use D9

Choose D9 when you need:

- **Driver-behaviour compliance** for commercial fleets (taxi, ride-hailing, logistics, delivery).
- **Video evidence** of incidents — both outside the vehicle and inside the cabin.
- **AI-based active safety** alerts that warn the driver *before* an incident, not just after.
- **National-standard protocol support** (JT/T808 / JT/T1078) that works with the Traqcare video pipeline.
